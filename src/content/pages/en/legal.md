## Nature of this petition

The European Public Order Initiative is an independent, non-partisan citizens' petition. It is **not** a European Citizens' Initiative registered under Regulation (EU) 2019/788, nor a petition to the European Parliament under Article 227 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union. It is **not affiliated with the European Union, any of its institutions, or any government, political party or company**.

The petition does not call for any illegal action. All its proposals are to be pursued through democratic, lawful processes, in full respect of national constitutions, the European Convention on Human Rights and the Charter of Fundamental Rights of the European Union.
